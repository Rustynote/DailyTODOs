Everything you need to plan your days

Add, complete & remove todos

Jot down a task, check it off when it's done, or delete it entirely — all in one click.

Drag and drop

Reorder todos or drag them onto a different day to reschedule instantly.

Auto-linked URLs

Paste a link into a todo and it becomes clickable automatically — no markup needed.

Inline image embeds

Image URLs are rendered right in the todo, so visual references stay in context.

Hide weekends

Declutter the calendar by hiding Saturday and Sunday whenever you don't need them.

Custom start of week

Set your week to start on Saturday, Sunday, or Monday — whatever fits your routine.

Import & export

Back up your todos to a JSON file, or import one to restore or move your data.

Clean up your data

Wipe everything and start fresh in one step, right from Settings.

Light & dark theme

Switch between light and dark mode to suit your eyes, any time of day.

Month, week & day views

Zoom out for the big picture or zoom in on today — switch views in one click.

Synced across tabs

Open the app in multiple tabs and your todos and settings stay in sync automatically.

Installable & offline-capable

Install it as an app and keep using it without a network connection.

Built with

A small, modern stack chosen to keep things fast and simple.

SvelteKitSvelte 5TypeScriptTailwind CSSVitePragmatic drag & dropService Worker

There is no backend. Really.

Everything is pre-rendered into static files and runs entirely in your browser. Your todos are saved to localStorage on your device — no server, no account, no sign-up, and nothing ever sent over the network.
